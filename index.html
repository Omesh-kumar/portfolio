Projects

Three problems, three different lessons about what breaks when a model meets the real world.

FlyRank Capstone
Content Opportunity Scoring

Trained a Random Forest to flag under-performing content at scale. The naive random split reported R²=0.882 — the honest GroupShuffleSplit reported 0.698. The 18-point gap is the finding: it's what data leakage costs you when pages from the same site show up in both train and test.
